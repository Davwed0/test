# Hong Kong Tax Categories

## Tax Deductions

### Charitable Donations
- Donations to approved charitable organizations
- Maximum 35% of assessable income
- Must have official donation receipts

### Home Loan Interest
- Interest paid on mortgage for self-occupied property
- Maximum HKD 100,000 per year
- Property must be in Hong Kong

### Elderly Residential Care Expenses
- Expenses for residential care of elderly family members
- Maximum HKD 100,000 per year
- Must be in approved residential care homes

### Mandatory Provident Fund (MPF)
- Mandatory contributions to MPF schemes
- Maximum HKD 18,000 per year
- Employee mandatory contributions

### Personal Allowances
- Basic allowance
- Married person's allowance
- Child allowance
- Dependent parent/grandparent allowance
- Single parent allowance

### Self-Education Expenses
- Course fees for prescribed education courses
- Maximum HKD 100,000 per year
- Must be job-related or career development

## Income Categories

### Salaries Income
- Employment income
- Wages, salary, leave pay
- Commission, bonus, gratuity
- Perquisites and benefits in kind
- Stock options and awards

### Rental Income
- Property rental income
- Income from letting of properties in Hong Kong
- Gross rental income less deductible expenses

### Business Profits
- Profits from trade, business, or profession
- Income from business operations
- Net profit after deductible expenses

### Investment Income
- Dividend income
- Interest income (excluding offshore interest)
- Investment gains

### Other Income
- Pension income
- Annuity income
- Royalty income
- Occasional income
